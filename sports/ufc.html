02 / ENDPOINTS

14 UFC endpoints

All routes are GET on https://ufc-api.propbetedge.ai. Public routes need no key; the rest take X-API-Key or ?key=.

  • GET

  • GET
    /v1/ufc/resultsResults · Public

FIGHTERS & BOUTS

  • GET
    /v1/ufc/fightersFighters · Public
